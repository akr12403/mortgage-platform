## Services

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mortgage-service` | 8080 | `/actuator/health` | `docker/Dockerfile.mortgage-service` | origination, approval stub, payments, delinquency rule; sole event publisher |
| `servicing-service` | 8081 | `/actuator/health` | `docker/Dockerfile.servicing-service` | consumes events → servicing history + delinquency snapshot |

## Runtime topology (docker compose)

`postgres` (17.7) → `db-migrate` (applies idempotent migrations 002+ on every `up`) and `localstack` (SNS/SQS) → `messaging-init` (topic, two consumer queues, two DLQs, subscriptions). The three services start only after both init jobs exit successfully. Their health comes from the Dockerfile `HEALTHCHECK`. Images are multi-stage, run as a non-root user, and carry no credentials. AWS settings and DB passwords are injected as environment variables at runtime.

## Cross-cutting rules

- Business IDs (`BOR-###`, `MTG-####`, `LOAN-####`) in all APIs and events; UUIDs stay internal.
- `X-Correlation-Id` is accepted or generated by every service, echoed in the response, written to every log line, copied into event envelopes, and restored into consumer logs.
- Errors use `{code, message, correlationId, timestamp}` with no stack traces.
- Events are published after commit; consumers are idempotent on `eventId` and acknowledge after commit; 3 receives → DLQ.

## Known limits

See the end of [m5-demo-checklist.md](../operations/m5-demo-checklist.md#known-limits-stated-not-hidden): shared database for reads, publish-after-commit instead of an outbox, in-memory dedupe in analytics, no auth until M6, and LocalStack without persistence.
