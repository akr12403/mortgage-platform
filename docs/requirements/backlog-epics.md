# Backlog Epics

### 1. Borrower Management
Enables the centralized registration, identity verification, and profile lifecycle tracking for mortgage applicants. Delivering this capability ensures accurate borrower metadata association across all downstream origination and servicing processes.

### 2. Mortgage Management
Handles primary loan origination workflows, application ingestion, and core mortgage state transitions. This epic provides the foundational domain entity models necessary to track a mortgage from initial submission through final approval.

### 3. Underwriting
Automates financial evaluation, debt-to-income (DTI) calculations, and policy compliance verification for loan applicants. Standardizing underwriting logic reduces manual decisioning overhead and improves loan risk assessment accuracy.

### 4. Servicing
Provides post-origination loan maintenance, loan schedule tracking, and escrow account balancing. This delivers core operational value by maintaining full visibility over active mortgage balances and interest accruals.

### 5. Payment
Processes incoming borrower payment transactions, principal/interest allocations, and late payment fee assessments. Establishing reliable payment logic ensures accurate ledger reconciliation and account balance updates.

### 6. Delinquency
Identifies overdue payment accounts, categorizes default severity stages, and structures workout or loss mitigation options. Early delinquency detection minimizes portfolio credit losses and improves workout tracking.

### 7. Analytics
Aggregates transactional events into real-time operational performance metrics, risk projections, and yield dashboards. This provides executive leadership with actionable portfolio insights without degrading primary transaction systems.

### 8. Document Management
Manages secure file uploads, metadata tagging, and text extraction for legal mortgage documentation. Centralizing document workflows simplifies audit compliance and accelerates automated policy indexing.

### 9. Security
Enforces authentication, authorization, secret management, and data encryption across all platform boundaries. Implementing zero-trust security safeguards sensitive financial transactions and enforces institutional compliance.

### 10. AI Assistant
Provides natural language querying, document context retrieval, and policy guidance through a specialized conversational agent. This empowers internal operators and borrowers with immediate, context-aware answers to complex mortgage guidelines.

### 11. Operations
Establishes deployment pipelines, containerized orchestration, application monitoring, and environment health checks. Robust platform operations ensure high availability, cross-platform parity, and predictable delivery across environments.
