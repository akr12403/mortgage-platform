# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder Role | Primary Interest | Influence Level | Capstone Touchpoint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Servicing Operations** | Payment tracking accuracy, borrower escrow management, and streamlined delinquency handling. | High | Servicing APIs, Payment Processing, and Delinquency Management modules. |
| **Portfolio Risk Manager** | Real-time visibility into loan portfolio yield, default risks, and financial performance metrics. | High | Mortgage Analytics Service, Event Bus streaming, and Reporting Dashboards. |
| **Compliance Officer** | Immutable audit logs, synthetic data isolation, regulatory standards, and zero plain-text credentials. | High | Security Architecture, Audit Loggers, and Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC). |
| **Underwriting Specialist** | Accurate debt-to-income (DTI) calculations, credit evaluation rules, and quick loan origination decisions. | Medium | Mortgage Service and Underwriting Decision Engine. |
| **Customer Support Agent** | Fast document retrieval, accurate borrower history lookups, and clear Q&A assistance. | Medium | AI Knowledge Assistant and Borrower Document Management. |
| **DevOps Administrator** | Cross-platform runtime parity, clean build pipelines, container stability, and deployment ease. | High | Docker Compose, Terraform scripts, and CI/CD workflows. |
